    For parents

    Our practice stories follow guided reading levels. Levels A to F match what most children read in their last preschool years and first year of primary school.

    What each level looks like

    LevelTypical ageOn the page
    AAge 4 to 5One short line per page with a repeated pattern: “Pip can run.”
    BAge 4 to 5The pattern changes a little; the picture carries the meaning.
    CAge 5 to 6Longer lines and more sight words on familiar topics.
    DAge 5 to 6Sentences vary, and characters start to talk.
    EAge 6 to 7Several lines per page and events that happen in order.
    FAge 6 to 7Longer sentences, quotation marks and a small problem to solve.

    Read it three ways

    First, let the story read itself while your child follows along. Next, read it together. Last, your child reads and taps only the words they get stuck on.

    Ask after the story

    Each book ends with one question to talk about. Talking about a story builds comprehension as much as decoding does.

    Private by design

    The stories, word game and reading log need no account. Minutes, stickers and finished books are saved only in this browser on this device.
