    For grown-ups

    Smart Reader Kids follows the guided reading levels many classrooms use. Levels A to F roughly cover kindergarten through the middle of first grade.

    What each level looks like

    LevelTypical gradeOn the page
    AKindergartenOne short line per page with a repeated pattern: “Pip can run.”
    BKindergartenThe pattern changes a little; the picture carries the meaning.
    CKindergartenLonger lines and more sight words on familiar topics.
    DK to 1stSentences vary, and characters start to talk.
    E1st gradeSeveral lines per page and events that happen in order.
    F1st gradeLonger sentences, quotation marks and a small problem to solve.

    Read it three ways

    First, let the story read itself while your child follows along. Next, read it together. Last, your child reads and taps only the words they get stuck on.

    Ask after the story

    Each book ends with one question to talk about. Talking about a story builds comprehension as much as decoding does.

    Private by design

    No accounts and no ads. Minutes, stickers and finished books are saved only in this browser on this device.
